# Provenance of the Lean sources

This file lists where each of the 413 Lean source files of this repository (`Challenge.lean`,
`Solution.lean` and every `.lean` file under `Complexity/`, `PT/` and `ArtGallery/`) comes from, as
of 2026-10-27. It was produced by comparing every file with the files of the source repositories
below (exact byte comparison, then a line diff after replacing `Chazelle` by `ArtGallery` in the
source, and a search for shared lines against every file of the three sources to find files that
were moved or split). Line counts are physical lines.

## Sources

* **NPCompleteness-Lean4** — <https://github.com/RBarish-UTokyo/NPCompleteness-Lean4>,
  commit `3e603e795b44fedcf7d6dd65b7d1e30547ead925` (Palomar entry
  [PALOMAR-2026-10-02-000013](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-10-02-000013&version=1) v1,
  *NP-completeness of SAT, 3-SAT and their variants (the Cook–Levin theorem) in Lean 4*),
  Apache-2.0: the machine model (`Complexity/Machine.lean`), P, NP and polynomial-time reductions
  (`Complexity/Classes.lean`), and the modules they and `PT/` depend on. According to that entry,
  its `Complexity` library was written by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI) and revised and extended by Claude
  Opus 5.5 (Anthropic).
* **Max3SAT-MaxSNP-APX-Lean4** — <https://github.com/RBarish-UTokyo/Max3SAT-MaxSNP-APX-Lean4>,
  commit `10079b2a14bd0df47d2a24364133f6008b9c207f` (Palomar entry
  [PALOMAR-2026-10-09-000010](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-10-09-000010&version=1) v1,
  *MaxSNP-completeness and APX-completeness of MAX-3-SAT and 3-OCCURRENCE MAX-3-SAT (the PCP
  theorem and hardness of approximation) in Lean 4*), Apache-2.0 (a later entry of the same
  author, which includes the NPCompleteness-Lean4 library; the 19 files taken from that library are
  byte-identical in both): the polynomial-time framework `PT/` (`PT f`, with `fun_prop` support),
  the register-machine language `Complexity/Reg.lean`, `Complexity/Reg/*`, and
  `Complexity/Approx/Defs.lean`, `Complexity/PCP/Defs.lean`. These were written for that entry by
  Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic). Its `Complexity/Reg/Clocked.lean`
  is not included. That repository also contains Lean files adapted from the OpenAI Math
  release (its `OAI/` directory); none of them is included or imported here.
* **LinearTimeTriangulation-ChazelleAlgorithm-Lean4** —
  <https://github.com/RBarish-UTokyo/LinearTimeTriangulation-ChazelleAlgorithm-Lean4>,
  commit `92cc7745aa026aad3e80e7a72fe88cfbd909614a` (Palomar entry
  [PALOMAR-2026-10-05-000003](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-10-05-000003&version=2) v2,
  *Linear-Time Triangulation of Simple Polygons (Chazelle's Algorithm) in Lean 4*), Apache-2.0
  (same author): the polygon definitions of its specification and part of
  its geometric library — planar geometry (`Chazelle/Geometry`), the Jordan curve theorem for
  polygons (`Chazelle/Jordan`, without `Winding.lean`), existence of triangulations
  (`Chazelle/Existence`) and the affine-invariance lemmas
  (`Chazelle/Hardening/Invariance/{Affine,Transport}.lean`). The namespace `Chazelle` is renamed to
  `ArtGallery` throughout, and each file carries a provenance note in its module docstring.
  According to that entry, it was written with AI assistance, mainly Claude (Anthropic).

No other Lean code is reused. Mathlib (`v4.35.0-rc2`) is the only dependency.

## Where the definitions of the statement come from

* `ArtGallery/Spec.lean` (the specification) imports the machine model, `Language`, `PolyTime` and
  `PolyRed` from `Complexity/Machine.lean` and `Complexity/Classes.lean`, the unchanged files of
  NPCompleteness-Lean4 ("reused verbatim from the earlier Palomar entries", as its docstring says).
* Its polygon definitions (`Point`, `segment`, `IsBoundedSet`, `Polygon`, `n`, `vtx`, `edge`,
  `boundary`, `commonEndpoints`, `Simple`, `filled`) are those of `Chazelle/Spec.lean` of the
  Chazelle entry, verbatim up to the namespace and two shortened docstrings. `det` and the
  triangulation definitions of that specification are not part of the statement; they are in
  `ArtGallery/Geometry/SpecExtra.lean`, for the reused library.
* Everything else in `ArtGallery/Spec.lean` is new: binary codes (`unary`, `binaryNat`, …), ETR
  formulas and the language `ETR`, `InER`/`ERHard`/`ERComplete`, ETR-INV (`ETRINV`), visibility
  and guard sets (`Sees`, `IsGuardSet`, `IsBoundaryGuardSet`, …), rational points and the art
  gallery languages `AGP`, `AGPPointBoundary` (the point-boundary variant of Stade),
  `AGPBoundaryPoint` and `AGPInt`.
* `Challenge.lean` was generated by a program (not included in this repository) and consists of a
  header, the definitions of `Complexity/Machine.lean` and `Complexity/Classes.lean` (definitions
  only, without their lemmas and `deriving` clauses), the body of `ArtGallery/Spec.lean`, and the
  theorem statements. `lake comparator` checks it against `Solution.lean`.

## Authorship of the new files

All 347 files marked *new* or *generated*, the adaptations of the 8 adapted files, and the
programs that generated the *generated* files were written for this project with the assistance of
Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), under the direction of the author. The generator programs (Python,
exact rational arithmetic) are not part of this repository; the generated files contain data and
certificates that the Lean kernel checks, and apart from their header comments they are as
generated.
