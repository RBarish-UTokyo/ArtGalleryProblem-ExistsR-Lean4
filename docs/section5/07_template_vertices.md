## Changes for the certificates (08 §11.2)

* **Inversion, `r_j` pocket mouth.**  The left mouth corner of the tilted
  pocket of `r_j` was `a + (−2, 1) = (119/10, 11/10)`.  It lies *above* the line from
  `q = (139/10, 1/10)` (value `y = 2`) through `ξ1`, so for `y ∈ (1.58, 2]` the guard of `r_j`
  did not see past `ξ1` and the umbra was not covered for `x y = 1` (e.g. `x = 1/2`, `y = 2`:
  the point just above the crossing `X(1/2, 2)` is seen by no guard).  Now the corner is
  `a + (−2, 3/5) = (119/10, 7/10)`.
* **Inversion, slots of `g` on a coarse grid.**  The slot tips are `q = g + s d` with
  `d = (−7, −4)` (slot a) resp. `(−1, −1)` (slot b) and `s` on the grid `1/400` (so the edge
  `q → k` still points exactly at `g`); the floor corners `k, k'` lie at most `1/200` below the
  floor line.  The common denominator of the chain drops from 85 to 37 bits
  (`D = 125373947600`; the certificates use `4 D`).
* **Add le, corner `z` of the z-block.**  `(−25/2, −1/10)` → `(−13, −2/5)`: with the old corner
  the guard of `r_i` (value `1/2`) saw part of `Γ` (05_gadgets.md).
